# Enterprise Sales Deck

This document is a ready-to-present `12-slide` sales deck for `Hefner Sprach Hub`.

Use it as:

- a presentation script
- a design handoff for slides
- the content source for PowerPoint, Google Slides, or PDF

## Slide 1. Title

Title:

`Hefner Sprach Hub`

Subtitle:

`Governed enterprise learning workspace for onboarding, upskilling, and internal knowledge transfer`

Footer:

- company name
- presenter
- date

Speaker note:

Present the platform as a modern internal academy operating model, not as a generic LMS.

## Slide 2. The problem

Title:

`Why enterprise learning breaks down`

Content:

- knowledge is spread across documents, meetings, recordings, and informal support
- onboarding takes too long because the learning path is not structured
- managers cannot see who is progressing, blocked, or ready
- publishing and release control are too weak for reliable internal learning operations

Speaker note:

Frame the problem around operational readiness and knowledge transfer, not only around content delivery.

## Slide 3. The product

Title:

`One workspace for learning, delivery, and governance`

Content:

- public learning discovery and course pages
- learner workspace with guided lesson flow
- instructor and cohort delivery surfaces
- admin control plane with governance, reporting, and release readiness

Speaker note:

Show that the product is not one screen. It is a platform with dedicated surfaces for each operating role.

## Slide 4. Learner experience

Title:

`Structured learning that drives action`

Content:

- step-based lesson flow: theory, concept, video, quiz, practice, summary
- interactive practice and AI support
- recordings and supporting materials
- progress visibility and guided next action

Speaker note:

Stress that the user always understands where they are, what to do next, and what is already complete.

## Slide 5. Content governance

Title:

`Governed content operations`

Content:

- lifecycle from draft to published
- release readiness checks
- version snapshots
- scoped accountability for academy, course, and cohort

Speaker note:

This is the bridge from course editing to enterprise-grade content operations.

## Slide 6. Completion evidence

Title:

`Completion you can prove`

Content:

- completion rules at course level
- attendance and learning thresholds
- learner attestation
- immutable completion evidence

Speaker note:

Position this as a platform for accountable learning, not just for content consumption.

## Slide 7. Visibility for owners and managers

Title:

`Operational visibility built into the platform`

Content:

- at-risk learner detection
- manager and compliance summary
- governance signals
- delivery health and readiness views

Speaker note:

The platform gives operational value to managers, academy owners, and delivery leads, not only to learners.

## Slide 8. Security and deployment

Title:

`Enterprise deployment foundation`

Content:

- split-stack deployment model
- isolated public, learner, teacher, and admin surfaces
- audit event foundation
- backup, restore, and retention package
- identity-provider foundation for enterprise rollout

Speaker note:

Be explicit: the platform already has an enterprise foundation, and optional controls can be activated per deployment.

## Slide 9. Ideal use cases

Title:

`Where the platform fits best`

Content:

- internal academies
- onboarding programs
- role-transition and upskilling tracks
- technical training
- compliance-ready internal learning
- distributed expert organizations

Speaker note:

Keep the message universal. Banking is one fit, not the only fit.

## Slide 10. Pilot structure

Title:

`Suggested pilot`

Content:

- `1` business unit or academy
- `100-300` learners
- `2-3` learning tracks
- `6-8` weeks
- success metrics on activation, completion, readiness, and governance quality

Speaker note:

The pilot is not a free trial. It is a measurable operational validation.

## Slide 11. Commercial model

Title:

`How we package the platform`

Content:

- implementation and setup fee
- annual platform fee
- optional enterprise identity activation
- optional AI activation
- optional branded private deployment
- optional ongoing professional services

Speaker note:

Anchor on recurring platform value, not one-time code transfer.

## Slide 12. Close

Title:

`Why this matters now`

Content:

- reduce time-to-readiness
- make internal knowledge reusable
- improve managerial visibility
- govern publishing and completion
- deploy an extensible enterprise learning platform, not a static LMS

Speaker note:

End on platform value and rollout readiness.
