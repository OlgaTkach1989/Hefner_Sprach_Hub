# Commercial One-Pager

## Product

`Learning Hub`

`Governed enterprise learning workspace for onboarding, upskilling, and internal knowledge transfer`

## What it solves

- fragmented internal knowledge
- slow onboarding
- weak visibility into learner readiness
- uncontrolled content publishing
- lack of completion evidence

## What the platform includes

- public course discovery
- learner workspace with guided lesson flow
- instructor and cohort delivery surfaces
- admin control plane
- release governance
- attestation and completion evidence
- manager and compliance reporting foundation

## Best-fit scenarios

- internal academies
- onboarding programs
- technical training
- role-transition learning
- compliance-ready internal learning

## Suggested pilot

- `100-300` learners
- `2-3` learning tracks
- `6-8` weeks
- measurable KPIs on activation, completion, and readiness

## Commercial structure

- implementation fee
- platform fee
- optional enterprise identity activation
- optional AI activation
- optional private branded deployment

## Recommended next step

Run a paid pilot and use it to validate:

- learner adoption
- manager visibility
- content governance
- readiness and completion evidence
