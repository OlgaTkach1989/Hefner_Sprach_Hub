# Vendor-Ready Deck Outline

Use this outline for a `10-12` slide enterprise sales deck.

## Slide 1. Title

`Learning Hub`

Subtitle:

`Governed enterprise learning workspace for onboarding, upskilling, and internal knowledge transfer`

## Slide 2. Problem

Key pain points:

- learning content is scattered across documents, meetings, and tribal knowledge
- onboarding takes too long
- managers lack visibility into readiness and completion
- publishing and evidence processes are not controlled enough

## Slide 3. Product

Show the platform as one operating model:

- public learning catalog
- structured learner workspace
- instructor and cohort delivery tools
- admin control plane

## Slide 4. Learner experience

Focus on:

- step-by-step lesson flow
- recordings and resources
- practice and AI assistance
- progress and attestation

## Slide 5. Content governance

Show:

- draft to publish lifecycle
- release readiness
- version history
- scoped accountability

## Slide 6. Completion evidence

Show:

- rule-based completion
- attendance thresholds
- attestation
- evidence issuance

## Slide 7. Operational visibility

Show:

- manager and compliance summary
- at-risk learners
- audit and exception signals
- delivery health

## Slide 8. Security and deployment

Show:

- split-stack deployment
- admin and workspace separation
- backups and restore model
- identity provider foundation

## Slide 9. Enterprise fit

Positioning:

- internal academies
- onboarding programs
- technical enablement
- compliance-ready learning
- distributed expert organizations

## Slide 10. Pilot offer

Suggested pilot:

- 1 business unit
- 100 to 300 learners
- 2 to 3 learning tracks
- 6 to 8 weeks
- measurable completion and readiness KPIs

## Slide 11. Commercial model

Suggested packaging:

- setup and implementation
- annual platform fee
- optional AI and enterprise identity activation
- optional customer-branded private deployment

## Slide 12. Why now

Close with:

- faster readiness
- controlled knowledge transfer
- better visibility for owners and managers
- extensible enterprise platform, not a static LMS
